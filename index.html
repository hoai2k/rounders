Left stick move · right stick aim · A jump · RT/RB shoot · LT/LB/B block

The Loop

Last one standing wins the round. Everyone who lost drafts a power card — all at once. First to the score limit takes the match.

Blocking

A well-timed block reflects bullets back at the shooter. Cards can make blocks dash, teleport, echo, or explode.

Mythic Cards

Magenta cards grant an active ability — trigger it with your block button (G / LB) when the pink ring is spinning.
