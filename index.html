Joining

Press any controller button (or a shoot key) to take a slot. ◀ ▶ picks your fighter, shoot or A locks in, B un-readies.

The Loop

Last one standing wins the round. Everyone who lost drafts a power card — all at once. First to the score limit takes the match.

Blocking

A well-timed block reflects bullets back at the shooter. Cards can make blocks dash, teleport, echo, or explode.

Pausing

Esc, P or the Menu button pauses. From there you can reach settings, controls, fullscreen, or quit to the main menu.

Paused

Music ducked. Nothing moves until you say so.
