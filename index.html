Local arena roguelite

Rounder

Four-player physics duels with loser drafts, volatile cards, controller rumble, and fast rematches.

Join: press any controller button. Start uses joined players only.

Settings

Controls

Keyboard P1

A/D move, W jump, F shoot, G block

Keyboard P2

Left/Right move, Up jump, / shoot, . block

Controller

Left stick/D-pad move, A jump, RT/RB shoot, LT/LB block

Draft

Move left/right and press shoot/jump to pick a card. Mythical cards activate with G or LB.
