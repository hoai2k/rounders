Images with an alpha channel pass through untouched. Images on a solid backdrop (magenta / green / white screen) get keyed out to transparent PNG. Nothing is cropped or resized, so body / weapon / arm parts stay on the same canvas.

Filenames route themselves: vex.png → characters/canonical/, vex_weapon.png → characters/render/. Keep the originals — drop them in art-source/characters/.
